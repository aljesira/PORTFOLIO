// About

From notebook to endpoint

I spend most of my time in the gap most teams underinvest in: the space between a model that scores well offline and one that survives production traffic. That means writing the eval before the architecture, instrumenting for drift before launch, and treating a 99.9% uptime target as a design constraint, not an afterthought.

Six years in, I've shipped fraud models that score transactions in real time, retrieval systems that cut support load by a third, and forecasting pipelines that inventory teams trust enough to act on without double-checking. I like problems where the data changes under you.
